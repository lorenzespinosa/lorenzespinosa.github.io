Jelly Workspace privacy policy

This policy covers the personal Google Drive integration operated by Lorenz Leslie Espinosa. It does not claim to describe every application or website operated by Lorenz.

Information accessed

  • Basic Google account information needed to verify the authorized identity, such as the primary email address and account identifier.
  • Drive metadata associated with files created by or selected for the app, including file identifiers, names, sizes, timestamps, and checksums when available.
  • Contents of files the owner explicitly selects for a requested upload, download, or restoration operation. Backup archives may contain personal or sensitive information chosen by the owner.

The current Google grant is limited to openid, userinfo.email, and drive.file. It does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or the entire Drive account.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the intended account, carry out owner-requested Drive and backup operations, check their results, and retain operational records needed for support and recovery. It is not sold or used by this app for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Use and transfer of Google API information is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Jelly Workspace itself has no model-training or fine-tuning pipeline.

Transfers and service providers

Selected backup files may be transferred between the owner’s machine and the owner’s Google Drive. Google processes data to operate its services. The assistant’s service providers, including the AI service used to interpret tool results, may process relevant identity or operational metadata to carry out an owner request. Provider processing is also subject to the applicable provider’s terms and privacy notices; this policy is not a claim that all data stays on one device.

For a backup upload, archive contents are transferred to Google Drive; publishing this support page does not publish those contents. This integration does not make Drive files publicly accessible merely because its OAuth app is published. File sharing changes require a separate owner-authorized operation.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and operational records are stored in the owner-controlled personal-agent environment. Backup copies are stored in the owner-selected Google Drive location. Credentials and records are retained as needed to operate and recover the integration, until the owner revokes, replaces, or removes them.

Backup retention and removal follow the owner’s instructions. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API use but does not erase existing Drive files, local archives, or recovery records.

Access removal and deletion

The owner can revoke the app through Google Account connections and can manage or delete stored backup files in Google Drive. For removal of app-held credentials or operational records, contact the operator. Identity and scope must be verified before a deletion is performed.

This public support website

The support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information to deliver the website, under its privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising, analytics tracker, Google login form, OAuth credentials, or backup data. A browser theme preference may be read from local storage to match the existing website’s appearance.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the integration’s Google permissions or data practices change. The effective date appears above.
